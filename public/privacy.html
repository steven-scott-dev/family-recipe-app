Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What we collect

Account info: your email address (used for sign-in). Family profile: family name, weekly grocery budget, and the details you enter for each family member — name, age, role (parent/child), dietary preferences, allergies, and disliked foods. Your activity: meal plans you generate, save, and load, star ratings per family member, and basic usage events (e.g. plan generated) that help us understand whether the app is working. We also count anonymous site visits (page viewed, referrer, a random session id stored in your browser, and basic engagement signals like scrolling, time on page, and button clicks — no IP address is stored) to see whether our outreach is working.

How we use it

To generate your meal plans, personalize them to your family's tastes and budget, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads.

Who processes your data

Supabase (database and authentication), Groq (AI meal generation — your dietary needs, allergies, ages, and budget are included in the prompts we send; never your email), Vercel (app hosting), and Kroger (only when you use the Send to Kroger cart feature — your shopping list items and chosen store are sent to Kroger so they can be added to your cart; you sign in with your own Kroger account, and we never see or store your Kroger password or payment information). Each processes data only to provide the service.

Children's privacy

SupperLine accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian. Any information about children (such as age or dietary needs) is provided by the parent — we do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we'll handle it.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and notify you in the app for material changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at @supperlineapp on Instagram.
